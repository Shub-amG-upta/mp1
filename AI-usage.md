## 📝 Summary of AI Assistance

- **C-Shell Implementation**: Guidance on POSIX syscall compliance (`fork`, `execve`, `pipe`, `dup2`, `tcsetpgrp`), lexer/parser grammar rules, and frecency algorithm scoring for `hop`.
- **xv6 MLFQ Scheduler**: System architecture for 4-queue MLFQ priority levels, time-slice tick accounting in `trap.c`, and anti-starvation priority boosting logic every 48 ticks.
- **Data & Plotting**: Visualization scripts (`plot_mlfq.py`) for rendering MLFQ timeline graphs and generating comparative benchmark tables.
